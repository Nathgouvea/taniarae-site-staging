Who is responsible

What I collect

Only what you choose to send through the contact form:

  • your name

Why I use it

To reply to your message and to arrange your free 20 minute call. I don’t use your details for newsletters or marketing, and I never sell or share them.

Your consent, which you give by ticking the box on the form (Article 6(1)(a) of the GDPR). You can withdraw it at any time by writing to me. This does not affect anything done before you withdrew it.

Your rights

You can ask me at any time to:

  • see the data I have about you (access)

Just write to [CONTACT_EMAIL]. If you think I have handled your data wrongly, you can complain to the Portuguese data protection authority, the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD).

This website uses no cookies, no analytics, no tracking and no third-party scripts. The fonts are hosted on this website, so visiting it sends nothing to Google or other font services.
